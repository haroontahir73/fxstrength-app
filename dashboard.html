Strength meter

Blended score from −100 to +100. Bars diverge from the centre line: right is bullish, left is bearish. The score is a medium-term bias; the chip beside it reads it against the last 5 trading days — on trend when price confirms the bias, poss. retracement when price is pulling back against it (a continuation watch, not a reversal call). Crowded = speculative net above 35% of open interest; COT extreme = speculative net at a multi-week high or low, where a trend change often starts (COT turning once it rolls over). Gold, silver and crude sit below the rule — own model, wider score range, not in the currency centring or pair ranking.
